REDEEMED LIFE MINISTRIES • SENIOR PASTOR & OVERSEER
HIS STORY

A Life Shaped
By Christ.

Pastor Mark Kiyaga is the visionary leader of Redeemed Life Ministries, serving as Senior Pastor and Overseer.

His journey with Christ began at the age of ten following a life-changing encounter with Jesus in June 1983. That encounter ignited a lifelong commitment to Christ and to the work of ministry.

Pastor Mark officially entered pastoral ministry in 1993 and received full ordination in 1997 after graduating from Makerere University, Kampala.

HIS MINISTRY

Preaching the Gospel.
Raising Disciples.

01

A Passion for Souls

Pastor Mark carries a deep passion for seeing people encounter Jesus Christ. His ministry is marked by a commitment to preaching the Gospel and calling people into a transformed life of faith.

02

Teaching God's Word

Beyond preaching, Pastor Mark is a profound teacher of the Word, committed to helping believers understand Scripture and grow in spiritual maturity.

03

Healing & Deliverance

As a conference speaker and crusade preacher, Pastor Mark ministers with a strong emphasis on faith, healing and deliverance through the power of Jesus Christ.

04

The Great Commission

His dedication to the Great Commission has taken him across Africa, preaching the Gospel and reaching people with the message of salvation through Christ.
