OUR LEADERSHIP

Shepherding With Faith.
Leading With Love.

Meet the leaders who serve Redeemed Life Church with humility, faith and a deep commitment to the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

01
SENIOR PASTOR & OVERSEER

Pastor Mark Kiyaga Tendo

Pastor Mark Kiyaga Tendo is the visionary leader of Redeemed Life Church, serving as Senior Pastor and Overseer. His lifelong commitment to Christ began at the age of ten after a life-changing encounter with Jesus in June 1983.

Entering pastoral ministry in 1993 and receiving full ordination in 1997, Pastor Mark Kiyaga Tendo has devoted his life to preaching the Gospel, teaching God's Word and raising disciples. His ministry carries a strong passion for souls and a burden to see lives transformed through Christ.

A conference speaker, crusade preacher and charismatic worshiper, Pastor Mark ministers across Africa with a particular passion for healing, deliverance and the Great Commission.

MEET PASTOR MARK →
02
MINISTRY LEADER & PASTOR'S WIFE

Margaret Kiyaga

Margaret Kiyaga serves alongside her husband, Pastor Mark Kiyaga, as they lead and nurture the family of Redeemed Life Ministries. Together, they have built a home and ministry shaped by faith, love, prayer and a deep commitment to God's calling.

As a wife, mother and ministry leader, Margaret brings a heart of compassion and encouragement to those around her. She has shared in the journey of raising their four children and nurturing many spiritual sons and daughters who serve alongside them in God's vineyard.

Her life and service reflect a quiet strength, grace and dedication to supporting the work of the Gospel and strengthening families within the church community.

OUR PURPOSE

A Living Church With A Purpose

Our vision and mission express who we are, what we are called to do, and the difference we desire to make through Jesus Christ.

🌍
OUR VISION

A Living Church. Speaking Life In Christ. Impacting Generations.

We envision a living church that is rooted in Christ, speaks life through His Word, and positively impacts generations.

We desire to be a church that is alive in faith, active in purpose, and committed to seeing lives transformed through Jesus Christ. We believe the life we have received in Christ is not meant to be kept to ourselves, but shared with others and passed on from one generation to another.

✝️
OUR MISSION

We Go. We Stand. We Speak Life.

ACTS 5:20

We go where God sends us. We stand firm in faith and in the truth of His Word. We speak life through our words and through our actions.

Our mission is to faithfully live out the Gospel of Jesus Christ by taking His message to people, standing boldly for truth, and demonstrating His love through practical action.

We go. We stand. We speak life — in word and action.
OUR MANDATE
