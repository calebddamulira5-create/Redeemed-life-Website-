ABOUT US

Discover Who We Are

A Christ-centred church committed to raising disciples, transforming lives and impacting communities through the Gospel.

Our Story

Redeemed Life Ministries exists to glorify God by proclaiming the Gospel of Jesus Christ and serving communities with love.

We are committed to building a church where people grow in faith, discover their purpose and experience God's transforming power.

Our Vision

To be a Christ-centred ministry that transforms lives, raises disciples and impacts communities for the glory of God.

Our Mission

To preach the Gospel of Jesus Christ, nurture believers, and serve people through love, faith and compassion.

OUR FOUNDATION

What We Believe

Our faith is built on the truth of God's Word and the saving power of Jesus Christ.

The Bible

We believe the Bible is the inspired Word of God and the foundation for faith and life.

Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus Christ is the Son of God, our Saviour, Lord and Redeemer.

The Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit empowers believers to live a victorious Christian life.

Salvation

We believe salvation comes through faith in Jesus Christ and God's grace.
